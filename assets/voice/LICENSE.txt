GO TO BED!!! - voice assets: licences and provenance
=====================================================

Generated audio (every *.ogg in this folder and in ../sfx/)
-----------------------------------------------------------
All clips are synthesised by the project's own scripts (tools/build-voices.sh, tools/build-sfx.sh) from
assets-src/dialogue/lines.yaml. No recordings, no third-party samples. The generated audio belongs to the project and
ships under the project's licence. The sound effects are pure sox/ffmpeg synthesis (noise, sines, filters) and carry no
third-party material at all.

Build-time tools (not shipped; no licence obligation attaches to the output)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- piper-tts 1.8.0 (Python package; METADATA: GPL-3.0-or-later, bundles g2pW under Apache-2.0). Used only to render WAVs.
- ffmpeg 6.1.1 (Ubuntu apt build, GPL) with the librubberband filter (GPL-2.0-or-later), sox 14.4.2 (GPL/LGPL).

Voices evaluated and NOT used (docs/06 §6: the build must fail on a non-permissive voice licence)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- en_US-amy-medium    dataset "https://github.com/MycroftAI/mimic3-voices, License: See URL" (unresolvable) and
                      fine-tuned from lessac (below). Was the planned kid base; swap back in VOICE_OF to A/B.
- en_US-lessac-medium dataset Blizzard 2013 Lessac: a per-licensee RESEARCH licence
                      (https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html). Not permissive.
- en_US-ryan-medium   dataset RyanSpeech, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Non-commercial: excluded.
- en_US-joe-medium    dataset CC0 but "Finetuned from U.S. English lessac voice". Excluded for the lessac lineage.
- en_US-hfc_female/male-medium  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and fine-tuned from lessac. Excluded.
The derivative status of a TTS model versus its training data (and of audio versus the model) is legally unsettled;
the choice above simply keeps every link in the chain public domain so the question never has to be answered.

Processing (docs/01 "Audio direction", docs/06 §4.1)
----------------------------------------------------
kids: asetrate x1.18 -> atempo 1/1.18 -> rubberband pitch 1.15 formant=preserved (+8 % pitch and a 150 Hz high-pass for
scream/whine, 5 Hz tremolo for the Tablet Zombie); Big Bean: rubberband pitch 0.85; narrator: untouched. All: two-pass
loudnorm to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP (SFX -18 / -1), 48 kHz, Opus 48 kbps (SFX 64 kbps) mono. Second takes (<id>_b.ogg) are
the same line at +3 % pitch with a prosody variation.
