GO TO BED!!!

Your kids won a cursed night-light. Every door in your living room is about to open. You have a toy dart blaster. It levels up.

Family Sheet (optional, fills the game with your details)

First time: run Space Setup in your headset settings so the game can find your doors and couch. Then open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and choose Add this page to your library from the browser menu.

Controls

Shoot: weapon-hand trigger · Reload: grab the blaster with your other hand and pump it (or press A/X) · Place a door: point your other hand at the bottom of a real door and pull its trigger · Edit doors in play: B/Y · Begin: trigger when the room is ready. Hands work too: pinch = trigger, fist = grab.
